## Email still converts better than everything else

For a solopreneur, email is usually the highest-ROI channel you own outright — no algorithm between you and your list. The eight platforms below range from simple newsletter tools to full CRM-backed automation, all with AI features (subject line generation, send-time optimization, content suggestions) built in.

## How to pick

If you already need a CRM to track leads, [HubSpot](/articles/hubspot)'s free tier bundles email marketing in for no extra cost — the natural starting point for most solopreneurs. If email is purely newsletter-shaped (no sales pipeline), Kit or Beehiiv are built specifically for solo creators. Mailchimp remains the safest default if you want the most documentation and integrations available.
