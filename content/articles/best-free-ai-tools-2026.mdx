## Free tiers worth knowing in detail

### Claude

Anthropic's Claude gives free users a daily allotment of messages against a genuinely capable model — no watered-down "lite" version. For solopreneurs, it's a strong fit for long documents: contracts, blog drafts, or messy notes you need turned into something coherent. The free tier resets daily rather than monthly, so heavy single-session use (drafting a whole newsletter in one sitting) is where it shines.

### ChatGPT

ChatGPT's free tier runs on GPT-4o mini with occasional access to the full GPT-4o model. It's the most recognizable AI assistant, with the largest plugin and integration ecosystem, which matters if you're already using tools that plug into it. <AffiliateButton slug="copy-ai" />

### Canva

Canva's free plan includes the full drag-and-drop editor, thousands of templates, and a limited number of Magic Studio (AI) credits per month. For solopreneurs making social graphics, thumbnails, or simple one-pagers, the free tier alone covers most needs — Magic Studio credits are the only thing that runs out.

### Copy.ai

2,000 words a month won't run a content calendar, but it's enough to test whether Copy.ai's brand-voice and template system fits how you write before paying. It's a fast way to draft ad copy, product descriptions, and outlines. <AffiliateButton slug="copy-ai" />

### Grammarly

The free tier covers spelling, grammar, and basic clarity suggestions — the baseline every solopreneur should have running in their browser regardless of what else they use. Tone rewrites and plagiarism detection are the main things gated behind Premium. <AffiliateButton slug="grammarly" />

## FAQ

**Do any of these require a credit card to start?**
No — all 15 tools listed here let you create a free account and start working without entering payment details.

**Will free tiers disappear or shrink?**
AI companies do adjust free-tier limits over time, usually downward as costs rise. None of these are guaranteed forever, but as of 2026 all are actively maintained and marketed as permanent free plans, not time-limited trials.

**What's the best free stack for a solopreneur just starting out?**
ChatGPT or Claude for drafting and thinking, Canva for visuals, Grammarly for editing, and Notion to keep it all organized — that combination covers writing, design, and organization without spending anything.

**When should I actually upgrade to a paid plan?**
When a free-tier limit (word count, message cap, credits) starts blocking real work on a weekly basis. Until then, the free tier is doing its job.
