## Shipping code solo means the AI is your whole team

If you're building your own product without engineers to lean on, AI coding tools do the job of a pair programmer, code reviewer, and documentation writer combined. The eight below cover the range from lightweight autocomplete to full AI-native editors built around agentic coding.

## How to pick

For most solo developers, [Cursor](/articles/cursor) is the highest-leverage choice — it's a full editor built around AI from the ground up, not autocomplete bolted onto an existing one. If you just want to keep your current editor and add AI completions, [GitHub Copilot](/articles/github-copilot) is the safer, more established option. Building UI specifically? v0 turns a text prompt into a working component faster than writing it by hand.
