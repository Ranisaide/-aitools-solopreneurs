## Why writers need more than one AI tool

No single AI writer does everything well — drafting, editing, and brand-voice consistency are different jobs, and the tools that excel at one often aren't the best at another. Most solo writers end up running two or three of these together: one for first drafts, one for line-editing, and one for keeping tone consistent across a body of work. Below are the eight worth knowing, ranked by how often solopreneurs actually reach for them.

## How to pick

If you write marketing copy for clients or your own offers, start with [Copy.ai](/articles/copy-ai) — the template library is built for exactly that, and the free 2,000 words/mo is enough to test it. If your writing is longer-form (blog posts, guides, newsletters), ChatGPT or Claude will get you further per dollar. Grammarly runs alongside whichever generator you pick — it's an editing layer, not a replacement for one.
