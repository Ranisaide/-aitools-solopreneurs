## Design work you can't outsource but shouldn't do by hand

As a solopreneur you're making social graphics, product mockups, and marketing visuals with no design team behind you. AI image and layout tools have closed most of the gap between "usable" and "actually good" — the eight below cover everything from quick social posts to production-grade illustration.

## How to pick

For general day-to-day design (social posts, thumbnails, one-pagers), [Canva](/articles/canva) covers the most ground on a free or low-cost plan. For artistic or hero images that need to stand out — book covers, ad creative — Midjourney's output quality is still the benchmark, even without a free tier. If you specifically need a logo or brand kit, Looka is purpose-built for that single job rather than general image generation.
